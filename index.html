I am a psychologist, systems thinking practitioner, and group psychotherapist.

As a qualitative researcher I am interested in people’s lived experiences and the stories they share. My work often integrates narrative, multimodal, creative and appreciative methods to explore how individuals and groups give meaning to their joys, difficulties, and dreams for the future.
